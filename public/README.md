# public/

Drop your portrait photo here as **`sameed.jpg`** (or `sameed.png` — then update the `src` in `src/components/About.tsx`).

Recommended:
- **Format:** JPG or WebP
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5 portrait (e.g. 800 × 1000 px)
- **File size:** under 250 KB after optimization
- **Subject framing:** head and shoulders, centered, with the top of the head about 8% from the top edge — the component crops to `object-top`.

Other static files (favicons, OG image, downloadable resume) belong in this folder too.
